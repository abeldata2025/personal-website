Trust & Safety • Platform Abuse

When AI Moderation Becomes a Weapon: Ethiopia's Mass Reporting Crisis

Question
Why were Ethiopian TikTok accounts with no prior violations suddenly hit by cascading enforcement?
Evidence
Creator reports of suspensions, shadowbans, and removals, including the case of Ethiopia's most-followed TikTok creator.
Takeaway
Coordinated mass reporting can exploit automated moderation in low-resource languages such as Amharic and Tigrigna. Claims of a "Reporting-as-a-Service" market are flagged as requiring verification.

OSINT • Cross-Platform Verification

False in 2021, Asserted in 2026: Eritrean Troops and the Politics of Verification

Question
How should verification handle a government that denied Eritrean troop presence in Tigray in 2021 and asserted it in 2026?
Evidence
A purposive sample of 104 items: 41 X posts, 28 Facebook posts, and 35 YouTube videos.
Takeaway
Platforms should treat conflict-party statements as claims requiring verification, not as authoritative sources.

About

From evidence
to escalation

I investigate disinformation, coordinated information manipulation, platform abuse, and influence operations. My work combines open-source intelligence (OSINT), digital evidence verification, and analytical reporting, with a growing focus on AI safety and safeguards.

From 2021 to 2025 I led Inform Africa and its fact-checking initiative, HaqCheck, Ethiopia's first IFCN Verified Signatory. I personally conducted investigations, triaged claims by urgency and potential harm, reviewed graphic material from the war in Tigray, and served as Inform Africa's principal contact in Meta's Trusted Partner program, preparing and tracking content, account, and imminent-harm escalations.

Since relocating to the Washington, DC area in 2025, I have continued independent research and publication. I am now pursuing a long-term career on U.S. trust and safety, threat-intelligence, and AI safeguards teams, contributing directly to investigative and analytical work.

My path into this work began with Zone Nine, the digital rights blogging collective I co-founded in Ethiopia, and continued through newsroom leadership at Addis Zeybe.
